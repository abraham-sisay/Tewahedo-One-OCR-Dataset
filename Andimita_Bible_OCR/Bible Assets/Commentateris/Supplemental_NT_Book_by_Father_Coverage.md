Found means at least one eligible additional English passage is included in the JSON. No source means no qualifying additional direct exposition was verified in the searched sources; it does not assert that no other source exists. Already collected marks the user’s excluded collections. — means outside the requested scope. These are selected additions, not complete verse coverage.

† Cyril’s Matthew material is compiler-mediated Catena Aurea text. Two entries are extracts from his letters; the Matthew 16:18 entry is explicitly spurious and is flagged in the JSON. No authenticated public-domain English translation of a separate Matthew fragment collection was verified.

Ephrem: only the English catena excerpts in Conybeare’s 1926 appendix are included. The fuller commentary in that appendix is Latin. Attribution labels and doubts in the English excerpts are retained. Three items expressly printed as Chrysostom are retained separately under excluded_source_items, outside the Ephrem book/verse index.

Clement: Fragments I–XII and Who Is the Rich Man are excluded. Latin sections and editorial elucidations are excluded. The 2 Thessalonians quotation located in Stromata V.3 is not counted as a verified direct exposition.

Cyril’s Romans, 1–2 Corinthians, and Hebrews: the identified scholarly English translation is David R. Maxwell’s 2022 edition, which the publisher identifies as the first English translation; no reuse license was verified. It is excluded.
